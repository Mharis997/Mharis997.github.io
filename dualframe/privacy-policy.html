DualFrame Privacy Policy

App: DualFrame (Android, package com.dualframe) · Effective: 26 November 2026

DualFrame is a camera app that records a vertical (9:16) and a horizontal (16:9) video or photo at the same time. This policy explains how the app handles your data. In short: DualFrame does not collect, transmit or share any personal or sensitive data. Everything you record stays on your phone.

1. Data we collect

None. DualFrame does not collect, store on any server, transmit, sell or share any personal data, sensitive data or device data. The app:

  • has no internet permission, so it cannot send data off your phone;
  • has no user accounts or sign-in;
  • contains no advertising, analytics, crash reporting or tracking software, and no third-party SDKs.

2. Permissions and why they are used

PermissionUsed for
CameraShowing the camera preview and recording the videos and photos you choose to take.
MicrophoneRecording sound for your videos.
NotificationsShowing a "recording" notification with a Stop button while a recording continues in the background.
Foreground service (camera, microphone)Letting a recording you started continue when you leave the app or turn off the screen.

Camera images and sound are processed only on your phone, only while you are using the camera, and are never uploaded. You can withdraw any permission at any time in Android Settings → Apps → DualFrame → Permissions.

3. Where your recordings are stored

Videos and photos are saved only in your phone's own storage, in Movies/DualFrame and Pictures/DualFrame. Settings (such as video quality and frame rate) are saved only inside the app on your phone. The developer has no access to any of it.

4. Sharing with third parties

DualFrame does not share data with anyone: no advertisers, analytics providers, data brokers or other third parties. If you choose to share a recording using the app's Share button, Android sends that file to the app you pick (for example a messaging or social media app). That transfer is started by you, and that app's own privacy policy applies to it.

5. Data retention and deletion

Your recordings stay on your phone until you delete them. You can delete them at any time from DualFrame's gallery, your phone's gallery or a file manager. Uninstalling DualFrame removes its settings. It does not delete your recordings, so you don't lose them by accident. Because no data is ever collected, there is no data held by the developer to request or delete.

6. Security

All data stays on your device and is protected by Android's own security, such as your screen lock and device encryption. No data is transmitted, so no data is exposed in transit.

7. Children

DualFrame is not directed at children under 13 and collects no information from anyone, including children.

If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted at this address with a new effective date.
